CleanDrain connects residents with authorized barangay personnel so drainage problems can be reported, monitored, updated, and resolved transparently.

How CleanDrain Works

A simple community reporting process.

📷

1. Submit Evidence

Provide the location, description, contact information, and photo evidence.

🤖

2. AI Assistance

The prototype categorizes the drainage problem and suggests a severity level.

🛡️

3. Barangay Review

Authorized personnel inspect the report and update its progress.

✅

4. Track Resolution

Residents can monitor updates, photos, and the current report status.
